Most people save what's left after rent and bills. That's backwards. The surplus never shows up. Flip it. The day your salary lands, a fixed rupee amount auto-invests — before you can spend it. Pay yourself first. That's an auto SIP: one standing instruction. Willpower fails on a Friday night. A standing instruction does not. You never have to remember, bargain, or skip just this month. Because you buy on a schedule, not a mood, you average your buys over time. Quiet. Automatic. That's how the habit builds wealth. This week, set one auto SIP for a number you can live with. Then leave it alone. Educational, not investment advice.
