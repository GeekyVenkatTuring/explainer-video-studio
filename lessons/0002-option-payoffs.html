Buy-right
or sell-right?

Your next skill is to match an opinion to the contract language—before looking at a premium.

Call (CE): right to buy

A call buyer benefits when the underlying rises enough. The buyer may buy at the strike price; they paid a premium for that right.

Memory cue: call = “call it up.”

Put (PE): right to sell

A put buyer benefits when the underlying falls enough. The buyer may sell at the strike price; they paid a premium for that right.

Memory cue: put = “put it down.”

“Enough” matters: the option premium is a cost. A call can be directionally right but still lose money if the rise is too small or too late.

Fast check

You want protection against a fall in an index you already own. Which option gives its buyer the right to sell at a fixed price?

Choose one.
Key sentence: A call buys a right to buy; a put buys a right to sell.
