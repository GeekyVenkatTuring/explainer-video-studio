# Indian Futures & Options Resources

## Knowledge

- [SEBI — Module on Derivative Segment](https://investor.sebi.gov.in/pdf/reference-material/ppt/PPT-23-DMM-1.pdf)
  Regulator-produced overview of futures, options, margin, settlement, and risk management. Use for: core concepts and India-specific market structure.
- [NSE — About Equity Derivatives](https://www.nseindia.com/static/products-services/about-equity-derivatives)
  Exchange explanation of equity F&O contracts and their mechanics. Use for: checking contract basics and terminology.
- [SEBI — Risk disclosure for individual equity F&O traders](https://www.sebi.gov.in/legal/circulars/may-2023/risk-disclosure-with-respect-to-trading-by-individual-traders-in-equity-futures-and-options-segment_71426.html)
  Mandatory risk-disclosure context. Use for: keeping real-world loss risk front and centre.
- [SEBI — FY22–FY24 F&O profitability study release](https://www.sebi.gov.in/media-and-notifications/press-releases/sep-2024/updated-sebi-study-reveals-93-of-individual-traders-incurred-losses-in-equity-fando-between-fy22-and-fy24-aggregate-losses-exceed-1-8-lakh-crores-over-three-years_86906.html)
  Reports that 93% of individual traders incurred losses over the studied period. Use for: calibrating expectations—not predicting an individual's result.
- [NSE Academy — Basics Future and Options Training](https://www.nseindia.com/static/learn/online-courses-certifications-live-modern-financial-instruments)
  Structured exchange-linked curriculum. Use for: deeper, paced practice after the foundational lessons.
