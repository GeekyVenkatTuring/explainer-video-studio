Read the label
before the price.

A derivative is not just “NIFTY option.” It is a specific contract with an underlying, expiry, strike, and type.

NIFTY · 30 SEP · 25,000 · CE
NIFTYThe underlying index.
30 SEPThe expiry date. The contract has a deadline.
25,000The strike—the fixed buy/sell price in the option.
CECall European: a call option. “PE” means put European.

Never assume a contract's expiry or lot size from memory. NSE changes contract specifications and publishes the current details.

ABC · 28 OCT · 1,200 · PE — which statement is true?

Choose one.

Skill check: Before every simulated trade, write all four fields in a journal. If one is unknown, you do not have a trade yet.
