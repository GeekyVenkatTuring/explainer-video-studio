Lesson 4 · 8 minutes

A small premium
can mean a big bet.

Prices are quoted per unit. You trade a lot: a fixed number of units. Your risk is the quoted price multiplied by the current lot size—not the quoted price alone.

Option premium × lot size = premium paid per contract

Illustration only: Premium ₹120 × a hypothetical 65-unit lot = ₹7,800 paid for one option contract, before charges. A change of ₹20 in that premium changes the position by ₹1,300.

Underlying move × lot size = futures P&L change

For a hypothetical 65-unit futures contract, a 100-point adverse move changes P&L by ₹6,500. Futures use margin as security; margin is not a cap on loss.

Non-negotiable habit: Look up the live lot size, expiry, and contract rules on NSE each time. Exchange specifications and lots can change.
