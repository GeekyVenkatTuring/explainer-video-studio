# Money-Habit-60s — Screenplay

**Topic:** The one money habit that quietly builds wealth.  
**Core:** Automate investing on salary day (pay yourself first / auto-SIP).  
**Voice:** Indian salaried investor. ₹ framing. No picks, no return %, no invented numbers.  
**Length:** 110 spoken words · narration audio 58.7s (en-IN-NeerjaNeural, rate −4%). A 154-word draft ran 76.6s, so duration was hit by cutting words. Closer: “Educational, not investment advice.”

---

## 1. HOOK

**Narration:**  
Most people save what's left after rent and bills. That's backwards. The surplus never shows up.

**On-screen text:**  
- Save what's left?  
- That's backwards

**Shot intent:** Person at a café tapping UPI after a spend, end-of-month tightness — wallet or app looking empty.

---

## 2. FLIP

**Narration:**  
Flip it. The day your salary lands, a fixed rupee amount auto-invests — before you can spend it. Pay yourself first. That's an auto SIP: one standing instruction.

**On-screen text:**  
- Flip the order  
- Salary day → auto-invest  
- Pay yourself first

**Shot intent:** Young professional checking a banking or investing app on salary day; a debit notification lands, they look relieved not tempted.

---

## 3. PROOF-1 — willpower

**Narration:**  
Willpower fails on a Friday night. A standing instruction does not. You never have to remember, bargain, or skip just this month.

**On-screen text:**  
- Willpower fails  
- Systems don't

**Shot intent:** Weekend temptation (friends, shopping, phone in hand) against someone who is not opening the app to “decide.”

---

## 4. PROOF-2 — averaging over time

**Narration:**  
Because you buy on a schedule, not a mood, you average your buys over time. Quiet. Automatic. That's how the habit builds wealth.

**On-screen text:**  
- Buy on a schedule  
- Average over time

**Shot intent:** Same person, different days or clothes, repeating a small phone-check ritual; time passing, no drama.

---

## 5. CTA

**Narration:**  
This week, set one auto SIP for a number you can live with. Then leave it alone. Educational, not investment advice.

**On-screen text:**  
- Set one auto-SIP  
- This week  
- Educational, not advice

**Shot intent:** Hands confirming a standing instruction on a phone, then putting the phone down and walking on — done.
