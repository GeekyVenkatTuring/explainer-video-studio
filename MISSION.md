# Mission: Learn Indian Futures & Options safely

## Why
Build enough practical understanding to decide whether derivatives fit the user's investing goals, and—only if they do—to use a small, defined-risk position deliberately rather than follow tips or chase quick gains.

## Success looks like
- Explain futures, calls, puts, premiums, expiry, lots, and margin in plain language.
- Read a basic payoff and state the maximum possible loss before any simulated trade.
- Use paper trading and a written risk checklist before considering a real F&O position.

## Constraints
- India-focused, evidence-led learning using SEBI and NSE materials.
- Treat F&O as a high-risk trading/hedging tool, not a core long-term investment plan.
- Start with simulations; no recommendations or real-money trade instructions.

## Out of scope
- Intraday tips, “sure-shot” strategies, paid signal groups, and leveraged real-money trading while fundamentals are incomplete.
