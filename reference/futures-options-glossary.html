F&O quick glossary

Underlying
The thing the contract refers to: for example, an index or a stock.
Future
A standardised agreement: both sides have an obligation. Gains and losses move as the underlying price moves.
Option
A contract that gives its buyer a right, not an obligation. The seller takes the obligation if the buyer exercises.
Call
A right to buy the underlying at the strike price.
Put
A right to sell the underlying at the strike price.
Strike price
The pre-agreed price in an option contract.
Premium
The upfront price paid by the option buyer to the seller.
Expiry
The final date governed by the contract. Options lose time value as expiry approaches.
Lot size
The fixed number of units per derivative contract; it changes by contract and must be checked on the exchange.
Margin
Funds a futures trader (and many option sellers) must maintain as security. It is not the most the trader can lose.

Definitions simplified for learning. Verify live contract details on NSE before any order.
